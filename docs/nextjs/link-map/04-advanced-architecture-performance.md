# Link map · Next.js · Part 4 (chapters 11–14)

Covers every topic row of chapters 11 to 14 in `docs/nextjs/syllabus/04-advanced-architecture-performance.md`: performance and Turbopack (T11-01 to T11-06), SEO, metadata and accessibility (T12-01 to T12-05), testing and developer experience (T13-01 to T13-04), and agent-driven development (T14-01 to T14-06). That is 21 topic rows. The four "Project Milestone" bullets (T11-07, T12-06, T13-05, T14-07) are not topics and have no block. Every row has a written page of ours (a chunked topic shows its first chunk file).
*read* = I read the passage that supports the claim; *headings only* = fetched at 200, headings or opening checked. Third-party links carry a date where the page shows one. No `#anchors` are given. Where no good non-official explanation turned up after searching, the block has no "Beyond the docs" line. Two pages (github.com for `next-devtools-mcp` and `next-skills`, and the npm page for `@next/playwright`) refuse plain scripted requests and are not linked; the Next.js docs that describe them are.

🔴 **Version note.** We target **Next.js 16.3 and React 19.2**. Official links point at the unversioned `nextjs.org/docs/app/...` pages. Fetched on 2026-10-14, most showed `lastUpdated` dates between 2026-02 and 2026-10, some stamped version 16.4.0, with older outliers named in their blocks (accessibility 2024-11-06, `typedRoutes` 2025-08-19, Metadata Files 2025-10-17). The two 16.3 blog posts cited (Turbopack, AI improvements) carry a preview notice in their source, so the docs pages are the safer citation. Third-party picks are mostly written for Next.js 14 or 15 and say so in their block. Three syllabus flags shape the picks: the Rust React Compiler is **experimental** while `reactCompiler: true` is stable (T11-01); `runtime = 'edge'` is **deprecated** in route files and `preferredRegion` is marked deprecated, and Vercel's own page goes further and says 16.3 no longer supports `'edge'` (T11-04); and the earlier knowledge Skills are being **retired** while new workflow Skills shipped (T14-04).
