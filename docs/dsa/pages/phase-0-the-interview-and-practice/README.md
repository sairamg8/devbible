**Before any algorithm: what the coding round actually grades, the method that keeps you moving
when a problem is unfamiliar, and the practice system that turns three months into a result.**
Most failed rounds are failures of method, not of knowledge — the candidate knew the pattern and
did not find it in time, or found it and could not say why it worked, or wrote it and never tested
it. This phase is the method; the language-level implementations live in the
[JavaScript track's DSA phases](../../../javascript/pages/phase-15-algorithm-patterns/README.md)
and [Java's collections phase](../../../java/pages/phase-3-generics-collections/README.md), and
this track links to them rather than re-teaching them.

## Phase gate

Move on when you can run the full method on an unfamiliar medium problem in forty-five minutes —
clarify, brute force, optimise, code, test, complexity — while talking, and your mistake log has
its first ten entries.
