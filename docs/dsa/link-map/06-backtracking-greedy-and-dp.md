# Link map · DSA · Part 6 (phases 14–16)

Covers every topic row of phases 14, 15 and 16 in `docs/dsa/syllabus/06-backtracking-greedy-and-dp.md`: backtracking (T14-01 to T14-13), greedy (T15-01 to T15-14) and dynamic programming (T16-01 to T16-26). That is 53 rows. Phase 2 of `docs/dsa/pages` holds the backtracking skeleton chapter (06 to 06k), the bitmask chapters and the memoisation and modulus pages, so 10 rows are marked written and the other 43 are marked not written with a delta that describes what the syllabus row promises.
*read* = I read the passage that supports the claim on the fetched page; *headings only* = fetched at HTTP 200 and the title checked, passage not read for the claim. Third-party links carry a date only where the page shows one. No `#anchors` are given. Where no good non-official explanation turned up, the block has no "Beyond the docs" line. Official means language documentation, university course notes, cp-algorithms and the Wikipedia articles that cite CLRS; tutorial sites count as beyond the docs.
